How Consumer Quietly Outperformed in a Down Market

July 16, 2025

2022 was a reckoning. Valuations collapsed, growth dried up and for a while, even the word “venture” sounded like a liability. When the market turned, the noise faded, what remained was signal.

But not everywhere. While tech grabbed the headlines and capital got cautious, some companies kept moving—quietly, with discipline.

They weren’t chasing press hits or influencer campaigns. They were shipping product, to real customers, at real margins, and those are the type of companies we back. UNImade sustainability feel like luxury—refillable, minimal, and unmistakably modern. Goodles took mac & cheese off autopilot and rebuilt it with real nutrition, better flavor, and a brand people want to wear. First Day rethought kids’ health from the ground up, creating supplements parents actually trust and reorder. BeatBox took boxed wine, added culture and charisma, and broke into mass retail with speed. Literati turned book clubs into a lifestyle, delivering curated reading experiences that stick. Caraway made cookware aspirational, combining safety, design, and direct traction that dominated countertops and social feeds.

These brands are not trying to be everything. They focused on being better—and they delivered.

And then there were the ones that, pre-dated our firm, made it all the way through: Siete proved that heritage and culture can build a platform and exit without compromise. Simple Mills scaled clean-label eating before it was cool and earned a spot in every pantry. Poppi didn’t just disrupt soda—they reframed it entirely, and won.

None of it was luck! These outcomes came down to taste, timing, execution—and showing up early when others hesitated. While much of the market and “tourist tech investors” pulled back out of consumer, we kept leaning in, we underwrote margin, repeat purchase, and velocity. We didn’t bet on noise, we backed traction.

And when the market stumbled, these companies didn’t stall they kept building. Some got louder, some broke out, but all kept moving.

Now, three years later, the market is still uncertain, tariffs are punishing, rates are high, capital is expensive, every dollar has to work harder. The headlines are louder than ever, but the fundamentals haven’t changed. Product still matters, margins matter more than ever. Brands that move with focus and discipline—the ones that win trust day in and day out, the ones that pay attention to business fundamentals and build culture and community—are the ones that endure.

Goodles will keep growing because they’ve built real community, not just viral moments. First Day will keep stacking subscribers because trust in the product earns loyalty. BeatBox will keep taking fridge space because they outpace legacy brands with speed and relevance. Caraway will keep setting the standard in home goods because trust and design always matter. Uni will endure because sustainability coupled with luxury isn’t a trend—it’s a baseline expectation now. Literati will stay sticky because reading isn’t a transaction—it’s a habit.

We’re not waiting for the market to get easier, we’re building for the reality that’s already here. Because in real markets, execution wins and that’s how Springdale companies make it through – Not with noise, but with grit and staying power.

If you’re building something with staying power—we should talk.
