Hello

I’m Genevieve.

I co-founded Springdale Ventures, an early-stage consumer fund, after years building my own brands and running a consumer brand accelerator.

Before that: I grew up in Waco and on a ranch in central Texas, lived on a sailboat and in Mexico, spent five years on the ghats in Varanasi India and studying ethnobotany in South America. Now I invest from a ranch outside Santa Fe, occasionally write a newsletter called Dirt and Stars, and spend my mornings with horses who don’t care what I do for a living.
